ZenSnap

Mindful Custom Photo Puzzles

ZenSnap is a calm, minimalist photo-puzzle game for adults. Turn a muted scene — or your own photo — into a jigsaw and rebuild it at your own pace, across five modes (Classic, Rotation, Blind, Slide, and Master) and grids from 4×6 up to 15×15. Your photos stay on your device.

When you feel like measuring yourself against everyone else, the leaderboards rank the fastest solves and the fewest moves for every size and mode — under a display name you pick, with no account and no sign-in. Your times are kept on the device as well, and Settings can erase everything you have published in one tap.
